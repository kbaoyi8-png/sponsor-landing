The Problem

Field marketing runs on spreadsheets and Slack threads — not strategy.

When you're running ten, twenty, or thirty events a year, the coordination cost is crushing. You're chasing vendors, reformatting briefs, updating trackers, syncing sales, and rebuilding the same runbooks from scratch — every single time.

That's not a bandwidth problem. It's an infrastructure problem. Field marketing deserves the same operational leverage that engineering and finance take for granted. Spensor is that infrastructure.

How Spensor Works

An agent that thinks in events, not tasks.

01 / INGEST

Brief it once.

Give Spensor your event brief — dates, goals, audience, budget, venue context. It parses intent, not format. Drop in a doc, paste a Slack thread, or type it free-form.

Event brief → structured context
02 / ORCHESTRATE

Spensor drafts the runbook, identifies vendors and stakeholders, schedules outreach, flags dependencies, and surfaces blockers — before they become fires. Your calendar stays clear.

Tasks → timelines → owner assignments
